m-Reshammitra Mobile App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16 November 2026
Last Updated: 16 November 2026

1. Introduction

m-Reshammitra Mobile App is an application developed for supporting household surveys, geographical identification of households and monitoring of survey progress under the Government of Madhya Pradesh.

The application supports integration with the Samagra Family Register, on-site household surveys, GPS-based household identification, offline data collection and real-time progress updates for better analysis and decision-making.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, accessed, used, stored and protected through the application.

2. Information We Collect

The application may collect and process the following information for official survey purposes:

  • Household information collected during on-site surveys.
  • Relevant information integrated with the Samagra Family Register.
  • Survey responses and household records.
  • GPS coordinates, including latitude and longitude.
  • Photographs captured during household surveys.
  • Relevant date, time and survey synchronization details.
  • Technical information required for application functionality and troubleshooting.

3. Camera Permission

The application requires camera access to capture photographs during household survey activities.

Camera access is used for:

  • Capturing household survey photographs.
  • Documenting on-site survey activities.
  • Supporting verification of survey records.
  • Associating photographs with relevant survey information.

The camera is used for application-related survey activities and is not intended for continuous or unauthorized background photography.

4. Location Permission (GPS)

The application accesses the device's GPS/location services to identify the geographical location of surveyed households.

Location information is used for:

  • Recording household geographical coordinates.
  • Geo-tagging photographs captured during surveys.
  • Identifying surveyed household locations.
  • Supporting geographical mapping and verification.
  • Monitoring and analyzing survey coverage.

Location information is collected for official survey activities and is not intended for unrelated tracking.

5. Internet and Network Permissions

Internet and network permissions are used to communicate with designated government servers, synchronize survey information and provide progress updates.

6. Offline Data Collection

m-Reshammitra Mobile App supports offline data collection to allow survey activities to continue in areas where internet connectivity is unavailable or limited.

During offline operation, survey information and related photographs may be stored locally on the device until an internet connection becomes available.

Once connectivity is restored, collected information is synchronized with the designated Government of Madhya Pradesh server.

7. Purpose of Information Collection

Collected information is used for:

  • Conducting household surveys.
  • Integration with the Samagra Family Register.
  • Household geographical identification.
  • Geo-tagging and verification of survey photographs.
  • Monitoring survey completion and progress.
  • Generating administrative reports.
  • Supporting planning and decision-making.
  • Maintaining official survey records.

8. Data Storage and Security

Survey information collected through the application is intended to be synchronized and maintained on the designated Government of Madhya Pradesh server.

Information collected during offline operation may temporarily remain stored on the user's device until successful synchronization.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or misuse.

9. Information Sharing

Information collected through the application may be accessed by authorized government departments and personnel responsible for survey administration, monitoring, verification and analysis.

Information may also be disclosed to competent government or law enforcement authorities where required by applicable laws and regulations.

10. Data Retention

Household survey records, GPS coordinates and photographs may be retained for the period required for official survey, administrative, monitoring and record-keeping purposes, subject to applicable government policies and legal requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

m-Reshammitra Mobile App is an official household survey application and is not designed or intended for children as its users.

Household survey information may include information relating to family members, including minors, where required for official survey purposes. Such information must be handled in accordance with applicable laws and government requirements.

12. User Permissions

Users can manage camera and location permissions through their Android device settings. Denying these permissions may affect features such as photograph capture and GPS-based geo-tagging.

13. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with designated government systems and authorized technical services to provide Samagra Family Register integration, data synchronization and other official application functions.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in application functionality, government requirements or applicable laws.

The latest version of this policy will be published on this webpage.
